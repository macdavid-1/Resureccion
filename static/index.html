Route all research traffic through your own device so sites see your phone's carrier/home IP instead of the server's datacenter IP — the reliable way past CAPTCHAs and bot walls. Run the one-file client (scripts/relay_client.py) on your phone (Termux) or any always-on home device:
python relay_client.py --server <this server URL> --token <token>
The device dials out — no port forwarding. If it goes offline, research continues through your configured proxy (or direct), and switches back automatically. Tracker/ad hosts are blocked before any request touches your device's network.
